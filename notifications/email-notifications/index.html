    Email Notifications

    Email notifications keep you and your team informed about your orders without having to check the platform. They are sent to the people in your workspace who have chosen to follow an order.

    If you want your software (rather than a person) to react to order updates, use Webhooks instead, or in addition.

    When emails are sent

    Notification type Sent when Email subject
    New delivery A new delivery is available for your order New delivery available for <AOI name>
    Status changes Your order is validated and moves to In Progress Your order for <AOI name> has been validated and will be fullfilled
    Status changes Your order is rejected Your order for <AOI name> has been rejected and will not be fullfilled
    Status changes Your order is Closed Your order for <AOI name> is now closed

    Who receives them

    Notification preferences are personal. Each workspace member decides for themselves which orders they want to hear about:

    • Email Notifications (main switch): Turns order emails on or off for you in the current workspace. When it's off, you don't receive any order emails from that workspace. Your per-order preferences are kept and apply again once you switch it back on.
    • Per-order preferences: For each order, you can turn on Status changes and/or New delivery emails independently.

    Other members' preferences are not affected by yours.

    Managing your preferences

    You can set or change your preferences in three places:

    1. When placing an order: in the Notification Preferences field of the New Order form.
    2. While viewing an order: using the bell icon in the top right of the order page in My Data.
    3. On the Notifications page: Account > Notifications contains the main switch and a searchable table with the preferences for all of your orders.
